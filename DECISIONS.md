- **Compensation terminology:** confirm “EMT time” versus the implemented “EMT Travel Time?” distinction if the supplied form's interpretation is challenged.
- **Operational ownership:** designated Office staff/primary DC, retention and disposal rules for sensitive files/SSN fragments, backup/restore ownership and final beta acceptance have not been fully established here.
- **Broader scope:** standalone compensation, City/payroll integrations, editable downloaded PDFs, residential Academy presets, Help & Instructions content and a broader definition of state-run courses require scope confirmation before implementation.
